Jeevamritham

Inspired by Subhash Palekar’s teachings, we learned that the manure and urine of a single desi cow is sufficient to fertilize one acre of land. Since Sun Green Farms now spans about 1.25 acres, we decided to care for two cows. They are mostly fed on grass grown within the farm itself.
Their dung and urine are used to prepare Jeevamrutham, which we apply to all our plants and trees once every 15 days. During summer, we also dry cow dung thoroughly until it turns powdery, then enrich it with Pseudomonas and Trichoderma for our own use, with any surplus available for sale. The impact has been remarkable—our coconut trees, after regular application of Jeevamrutham, have grown stronger and started yielding more coconuts year after year.

Our Jeevamrutham recipe follows the traditional method:

  • 10 kg desi cow dung
  • 7 liters desi cow urine
  • 2 kg pea flour
  • 2 kg jaggery
  • A handful of fertile soil
  • 200 liters of water
The mixture is kept in a drum, stirred clockwise twice daily for 5 days, and then applied to plants after diluting it in a 1:10 ratio with water.
