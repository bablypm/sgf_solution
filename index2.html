Welcome To Sun Green Farms
At Sun Green Farms, farming begins with the ecosystem. We enrich the soil with living microbes and trace elements, let natural cycles restore fertility, and allow biodiversity to thrive in every corner of the field. Each crop is grown in harmony with soil, water, and life around it — not against them. By respecting these natural relationships, we grow food that sustains both people and the planet, leaving the earth richer for generations to come.

Our Products
At Sun Green Natural Farm, we believe that true nourishment comes from working with nature, not against it. Nestled in the heart of Kerala, our farm follows sustainable and eco-friendly practices to grow a variety of natural products—free from harmful chemicals and rich in purity.
We are committed to restoring balance between agriculture and ecology by respecting the soil, conserving water, and encouraging biodiversity. Every crop we grow carries our promise of health, honesty, and harmony with the Earth.
Whether it’s traditional roots, wholesome grains, or natural remedies, our products are grown with care for your family and for the planet.
