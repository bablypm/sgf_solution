Welcome To Sun Green Farms

At Sun Green Farms, we believe that true nourishment comes from working with nature.
Nestled in the heart of Kerala, our farm follows sustainable and eco-friendly practices to grow a variety of natural products—free from harmful chemicals and rich in purity.
We are committed to restoring balance between agriculture and ecology by respecting the soil, conserving water, and encouraging biodiversity.

Our Products

Ranging from traditional roots and wholesome grains to natural remedies—are thoughtfully grown, carefully harvested, and meticulously processed, with deep respect for both your family’s well-being and the environment, while preserving timeless traditional values.
At Sun Green Farms, every product is entirely managed in-house—from seed selection and cultivation to harvesting, processing, and packaging. We offer only what we grow ourselves, ensuring uncompromised quality through nutrient-rich soil, chemical-free practices, and complete traceability.
All products are processed in a clean, dust-free facility built specifically for this purpose. We also use a solar dryer to help preserve the natural aroma and color of each product without compromise.

Why Choose Us

By choosing Sun Green Farms, you're not just supporting a farm—you're becoming part of a movement that values purity, ecology, and community. Together, we can reconnect with nature and rediscover the goodness of food grown with love and conscience.
  • Quality Organic Food Every product is cultivated with care, ensuring purity, taste, and the goodness of nature in every bite.
  • Chemical Free Our crops are nurtured naturally, without the use of harmful chemicals or synthetic fertilizers.
  • Clean & Green Our commitment to sustainable farming means every crop is grown naturally.
